About Taniti

Taniti is a tropical island known for natural beauty, adventure, and relaxation. Visitors enjoy pristine beaches, lush rainforests, and cultural experiences.

Cultural Insights

The island blends indigenous heritage with modern hospitality. Festivals, crafts, and local cuisine provide immersive experiences for visitors.

Travel Tips & Entry Info

  • ✈️ Arrival: Small airport; weekly cruise docking.
  • 💵 Currency: USD widely accepted; major credit cards welcome.
  • 🔌 Power: 120V outlets (US standard).
  • 🍻 Alcohol: Drinking age 18; no sales 12am-9am.
  • 🚶 Safety: Petty theft is rare; use standard precautions.
