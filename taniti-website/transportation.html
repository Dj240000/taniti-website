Transportation on Taniti Island

🚌 Public Bus Service

Reliable and affordable bus service connects all major areas of the island.

  • Runs from 5 AM to 11 PM daily
  • Multiple routes available
  • Air-conditioned buses
  • Stops at major attractions

🚕 Taxi Services

Licensed taxi services available throughout the island for convenient travel.

  • 24/7 availability
  • Professional drivers
  • Meter-based fares
  • Airport transfers available

🚗 Car & Bike Rentals

Explore the island at your own pace with rental vehicles.

  • Wide selection of vehicles
  • Daily and weekly rates
  • Insurance included
  • Free delivery to hotels

✈️ Getting to Taniti

Our island is easily accessible by air and sea.

  • The Taniti International Airport welcomes daily flights from major cities
  • Modern port facilities accommodate cruise ships and ferry services
  • Free shuttle services are available from the airport to major hotel areas
